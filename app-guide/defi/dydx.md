# dYdX in Vultisig

{% hint style="info" %}
dYdX vote is on the **Functions** button on dYdX in the **Wallet tab**, not the DeFi tab.
{% endhint %}

***

## How to vote

1. Open **Wallet tab** → **dYdX** → **Functions** → **Vote**
2. Enter the proposal ID
3. Select Yes / No / Abstain
4. Sign with your devices

You need DYDX in the vault to vote.
