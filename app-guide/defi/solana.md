# Solana DeFi in Vultisig

Solana staking and Kamino Earn are on the **DeFi tab**, not Wallet → Functions.

***

## Native staking

Stake SOL to validators through the Solana Stake program.

1. Open **DeFi tab** → **Solana**
2. Choose stake / deactivate / withdraw as offered
3. Enter the amount
4. Sign with your devices

Unstake (deactivate) and withdraw follow Solana's cooldown. The app shows the action that is available on the position.

***

## Kamino Earn

Kamino Earn is a Solana yield vault. The wallet can show a Kamino banner when the vault holds SOL.

1. Open **DeFi tab** → **Solana** (or the Kamino banner on the vault home)
2. Deposit the supported token
3. Withdraw when you want the position back
4. Sign each action with your devices

APY and deposited amounts come from Kamino. Read the position screen before you sign.
