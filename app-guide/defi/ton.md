# TON staking in Vultisig

{% hint style="info" %}
TON stake and unstake are on the **DeFi tab**, not Wallet → Functions.
{% endhint %}

Native TON staking. Stake TON to a validator.

***

## Available actions

| Action | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Stake | Stake TON to a validator |
| Unstake | Remove staked TON |

***

## How to stake

1. Open **DeFi tab** → **TON** → **Stake**
2. Select a validator (or use the prefilled default)
3. Enter the amount
4. Sign with your devices

## How to unstake

1. Open **DeFi tab** → **TON** → **Unstake**
2. Enter the amount
3. Sign with your devices

Unstaking may have a cooldown. Check the validator terms before you stake.
