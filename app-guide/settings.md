# Vultisig Settings

Open **Settings** from the vault home. Anything that belongs to this vault — backup, rename, reshare, Blockaid — is under **Vault Settings**.

***

## Vault

| Item | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Vault Settings** | Backup, rename, details, reshare, QR, GG20 upgrade, advanced |
| **WalletConnect** | In-app WalletConnect sessions on mobile. Pair a dApp QR here. Desktop dApps use the [browser extension](../vultisig-ecosystem/vultisig-extension/) |
| **$VULT fee tiers** | Current swap-fee discount for this vault. Tiers: [Swapping](wallet/swapping.md) |

***

## Address book

**Settings → Address book**

1. Add a name and address you send to often
2. On **Send**, pick the saved entry instead of pasting

The book lives on this app install. It is not a backup.

***

## Vault folders

Folders are on the **vault picker**, not in Settings.

1. Open the vault list
2. Add a folder
3. Put vaults in the folder

Folders only organize the list. They do not change signing or backups.

***

## On-chain security (Blockaid)

**Settings → Vault Settings → Advanced → On-chain security**

On by default. You can turn it off here. While it is on, send, swap, and WalletConnect / dApp screens can warn you about a malicious destination or contract. It is a warning, not a lock. You still decide whether to sign.

***
